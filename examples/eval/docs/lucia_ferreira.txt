Lucia Ferreira grew up in Seville and studied mechanical engineering. For six years she was an engineer at Nordwind Robotics, where she designed the arm of a warehouse robot.

In 2022 she moved to Madrid to lead a new team. Her first project there was the Aurora Drone, a small aircraft that maps fields from the air. The drone was released in 2023 and is now used on more than 300 farms.
