# Nordwind Robotics

## Company

Nordwind Robotics is a robot maker based in Hamburg. It was founded in 2008 by Klaus Berger, a
former ship engineer. Its main product is the Nordwind Carrier, a robot that moves boxes inside
warehouses.

## Ownership

In 2020 Nordwind Robotics was acquired by Baltic Logistics. The robot maker kept its name and its
factory in Hamburg.
