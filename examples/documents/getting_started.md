# Solar storage

Solar panels produce electricity during daylight hours. A battery stores excess electricity for use after sunset. Battery capacity determines how much energy can be stored.

## Charging

The charge controller limits the charging current. Dr. Patel recommends checking the battery temperature before charging. A warm battery may need a lower charging rate.

## Equipment

| Component | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Solar panel | Produces electricity |
| Battery | Stores electricity |
| Controller | Regulates charging |

# Maintenance

Inspect connections regularly. Keep the terminals clean and dry. Follow the equipment manual when replacing a component.
