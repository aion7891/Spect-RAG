# Tomas Ruiz

## Career

Tomas Ruiz is the chief executive of Aurora Labs. He co-founded the company in 2014 and has led
it alone since 2019. Before that he worked as a buyer for a supermarket chain in Lisbon.

## Expansion

In 2022 Aurora Labs opened an office in Madrid. The Madrid office is led by Lucia Ferreira and
builds software for large farms in Spain. Ruiz says the office doubled the number of customers
within two years.
