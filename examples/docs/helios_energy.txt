Helios Energy is a solar power company based in Porto. It was founded in 2011 and builds solar
farms for small towns in southern Europe.

In 2019 Helios Energy hired Maya Chen as chief technology officer. Under her lead the company
started using soil and weather sensors to plan where new solar panels should be placed. This
work led to the Helios Grid Planner, a tool released in 2021.

The Helios Grid Planner combines satellite images with ground sensor data. It predicts how much
energy a site will produce over a year. Engineers use it before they buy land for a new farm.

By 2023 Helios Energy operated 35 solar farms. Its largest site, near Évora, produces enough
electricity for about 60,000 homes.
