# River Valley Cooperative

## Members

River Valley Cooperative is a group of 120 family farms in the Douro valley, Portugal. The farms
grow grapes, olives, and vegetables. The cooperative was created in 2009 to share equipment and
sell produce together.

## Irrigation

Water is scarce in summer, so the cooperative installed Aurora S1 sensors on every member farm in
2018. Farmers check the readings in FieldView each morning. Since then the cooperative reports
using 30 percent less water while keeping the same harvest size.

## Plans

The cooperative plans to add the Aurora S2 sensor to track nitrogen in the soil. This should help
farmers use less fertiliser.
