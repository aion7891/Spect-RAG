# Aurora Labs

## 1. History

Aurora Labs was founded in 2014 in Lisbon by Dr. Maya Chen and Tomas Ruiz. The company began as a
small research group building low-cost sensors for greenhouse farms. In 2017 it released the
Aurora S1, a soil-moisture sensor that sold more than 40,000 units in its first year.

In 2019 Maya Chen left Aurora Labs. She later joined Helios Energy as its chief technology officer.
Tomas Ruiz became the sole chief executive after her departure.

## 2. Products

### 2.1 Sensors

The Aurora S1 measures soil moisture and temperature. The Aurora S2, released in 2020, added
nitrogen sensing. Both sensors send readings over a low-power radio link to a field gateway.

### 2.2 Software

FieldView is the company's dashboard. It shows sensor readings on a map and warns farmers when
soil becomes too dry. FieldView uses the irrigation model described in Section 2.1.

| Product   | Year | Main measurement     |
|-----------|------|----------------------|
| Aurora S1 | 2017 | Soil moisture        |
| Aurora S2 | 2020 | Moisture and nitrogen |
| FieldView | 2018 | Dashboard (software) |
